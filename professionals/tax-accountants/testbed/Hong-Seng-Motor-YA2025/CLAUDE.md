One client engagement of a tax practice. You draft, organise, extract, check and
summarise. The tax accountant decides: tax treatment, filing position, whether a

## Where things are

client sent · `03-Workpapers` work in progress · `04-Deliverables` final only ·
`05-Engagement-Admin` engagement letter, fees, the plan.

## How to work in this folder

Say where a fact came from. Never state a deadline as settled fact. Flag rather
than resolve. No figures in client-facing drafts. Nothing sends itself.

## Confidentiality rules

Do not repeat identity numbers, full account numbers or income figures in output.
Treat this folder as local.
