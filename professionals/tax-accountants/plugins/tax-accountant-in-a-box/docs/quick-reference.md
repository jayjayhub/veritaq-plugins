# Quick Reference

One page. Print it, or keep it open for the first fortnight.

You never need the skill names. Say what you want in your own words.

---

## Starting a new client

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"new client"* or *"run client intake"* | A few questions, then your folders and settings are written for you |
| *"plan this engagement"* | Proposes the document checklist, works out the schedule, drafts the first request |

Make the client folder first, named after the client, then run intake inside it.

---

## Chasing documents

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"who hasn't sent me their documents?"* | A list of every client and what each still owes |
| *"what's outstanding for Lim Trading?"* | That client's missing items |
| *"draft a follow-up for Lim Trading"* | A ready-to-send message, email and messaging versions |
| *"second reminder for Lim Trading"* | Same, but firmer. Four levels, up to a formal final notice |

---

## Deadlines

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"who's due in the next month?"* | Ranked by urgency, with who is not ready |
| *"who's at risk?"* | The ones where the cut-off has passed and documents are short |
| *"update my deadline calendar"* | Shows you a plan first, then writes it after you approve |
| *"when do I need Lim Trading's documents by?"* | The internal cut-off, not the filing deadline |

---

## Getting figures out of documents

Save what the client sent into their `02-Source-Documents` folder first.

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"extract the bank statements"* | A spreadsheet in `03-Workpapers`, every figure traceable to its source |
| *"pull the figures out of these invoices"* | Same, for whatever you point it at |
| *"what couldn't you read?"* | Every file it could not use, and why |

**Always check the `Control` sheet first.** If the totals reconcile, review is quick. If one fails, it tells you where to look.

---

## When something is wrong

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"check my setup"* | Diagnoses what is missing and how to fix it |
| *"what's missing for Lim Trading?"* | What that client's file still needs |

## The five folders

```
Client Name/
├── 01-Client-Communications   emails, chats, requests you sent
├── 02-Source-Documents        what they sent you, untouched
├── 03-Workpapers              your schedules, work in progress
├── 04-Deliverables            finished only
└── 05-Engagement-Admin        engagement letter, fees, the plan
```

---

## Four things it will not do

- Open email attachments. You save those yourself
- File anything. You submit through MyTax
- Read bad handwriting. It flags rather than guesses
- Decide a tax treatment. It asks; you answer

---

## Two habits worth forming

**Save attachments into `02-Source-Documents` as they arrive.** Everything downstream depends on this, and it takes seconds at the time.

**Tell it when a checklist is wrong.** Add the item it missed. Once something has come up for a second client it gets added to your standard list automatically, so the checklists get better the more you use them.
